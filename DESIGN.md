# Stockly Design System (DESIGN.md)

## Brand Identity & Context
Stockly adalah aplikasi manajemen stok & penjualan retail untuk UMKM.
Tone: Profesional, terpercaya, rapi, cepat, efisien.

## Palette & Colors
- **Primary Color:** Hijau Emerald / Teal (#0F7B6C / #00875A), aksen aktif, tombol primer, link aktif.
- **Surface & Background:**
  - Card & Container: Putih (#FFFFFF)
  - Page Background: Abu sangat muda (#F8FAFC / #F7F8FA)
  - Sidebar: Putih (#FFFFFF) dengan active state background hijau muda (#ECFDF5 / #E7F5EE)
- **Status Colors:**
  - Sukses / Lunas: Hijau (#059669 / #10B981) + soft background (#ECFDF5)
  - Pending / Warning: Kuning / Oranye (#D97706 / #F59E0B) + soft background (#FEF3C7)
  - Habis / Danger: Merah (#DC2626 / #EF4444) + soft background (#FEF2F2)
- **Borders & Lines:**
  - Border tipis abu halus (#E2E8F0 / #E5E7EB)

## Typography & Hierarchy
- **Font Family:** Sans-serif (Instrument Sans / Inter / ui-sans-serif)
- **Heading:** Bold, clean, tracking-tight.
- **Labels:** Huruf kecil atau uppercase muted/abu untuk metrik (TOTAL PRODUK, TOTAL STOK).
- **Numbers:** Tabular nums, bold, jelas terbaca.

## Components & Shape
- **Card & Panels:** Rounded corner (12px / rounded-xl), border 1px solid abu (#E2E8F0), subtle shadow.
- **Tables:** Header uppercase abu kecil, padding baris lega, subtle hover transition (#F9FAFB / #F8FAFC).
- **Buttons:**
  - Primary: bg-emerald-700 text-white rounded-md px-4 py-2 hover:bg-emerald-800
  - Secondary: bg-white border border-gray-300 text-gray-700 hover:bg-gray-50
- **Icons:** Outline style, konsisten 16-20px.
- **Navigation:**
  - Item aktif: background hijau muda + teks hijau + ikon hijau.

## Dials (Liveliness)
- ENERGY: 2 (balanced, purposeful for enterprise business app)
- RHYTHM: 2 (clean hierarchy with breathing room)
- MOTION: 1 (functional, subtle hover & transition states, no gratuitous bounce)
